After reading about the volumetric videos I wanted to try my hand at making my own gaussian splats. There are a handful of iOS apps available for this, and I decided to first play with Scaniverse.

After capturing a splat, you have the option of exporting the model (SPZ or PLT) or posting it to the Splativerse website to easily share with others. Check that out by clicking [here](https://https://scaniverse.com/scan/b6rm3m2tzmopuiga) .
