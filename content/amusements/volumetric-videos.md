I ran across this company [Gracia](https://gracia.ai) demonstrating volumetric video (4-D Gaussian Splatting) *streaming* from a browser using WebXR. In Apple Vision Pro, a click on their website -- without installing anything -- placed a musical artist in my space performing an intimate guitar ballad.

Even with the obvious technical limitations (for example, pass-through of my own environment on the AVP is a work-in-progress and will be available soon, according to their website and has already been implemented on the Meta Quest 3), experiencing this first-hand was a revelation.

One of the first immersive videos I watched the day I got my Apple Vision Pro (over two years ago now) was a performance by Alecia Keys with her band and crew in a rehearsal studio. I was floored by the intimacy of that experience, although it was highly curated by the editors. I sat in one spot as camera angles were chosen for me. I had leeway in moving my head left, right, up and down to focus on individual performers, Alecia's pedal work, the skylights on the ceiling, etc.

That immersive video was very impressive, and took my breath away the first time I watched it. But I still felt limited by the fact I couldn't choose my own camera angle, let alone walk through the studio and prop myself at the piano between the backup singers.

Soon we'll be able to do exactly that. Watching this demo last night was the first time that I got actual holodeck vibes. (Just don't try leaning on the piano, the force fields aren't quite there yet.)
